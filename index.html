Car Compatibility Checker

Support for the offline vehicle compatibility checker for Apple CarPlay.

Get help

If you found incorrect vehicle information, have trouble using the app, or want to suggest an improvement, email sugardevteam@gmail.com.

Before relying on a result

Availability can vary by country, model year, trim, options, and infotainment system. Confirm compatibility with the vehicle manufacturer or dealer before purchasing a vehicle or accessory.

Data and privacy

The app works offline and does not require an account. Saved checks and preferences remain on your device.
